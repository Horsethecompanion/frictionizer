How it works

One question. Every time.

You choose which apps to monitor — Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, whatever your weakness is. The next time you open one, this is what happens:

1

You tap the app

Frictionizer intercepts the moment the app opens — before anything loads.

2

A question appears

"What are you doing?" Five options. You must pick one before the app opens. No skip button.

3

A short countdown runs

You can't tap an answer until the countdown reaches zero — so you can't just muscle-memory past it. The wait starts at 1 second on day one and grows gradually from there.

4

The app opens

That's it. No blocking, no guilt, no data sent anywhere. Just a pause.

The boiling frog

It gets harder, slowly.

The countdown before you can pick an answer grows by one second every day, up to a 60-second cap. The idea: you barely notice it at first, and by the time it's a real pause, the habit of stopping to think is already there.

🐸

Gradual by design

You can reset the countdown in Settings if you want to — but you'll need to type a randomly generated 10-character code first. Just enough friction to make it a deliberate choice.

Installation

Installing outside the Play Store

Frictionizer isn't on the Play Store yet, so installation takes a few extra steps. It's straightforward — here's exactly what to do.

1

Download the APK to your phone

Tap the button below on your Android phone to download the installer file (called an APK). If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose Keep or Download anyway.

Download frictionizer.apk
2

Allow installation from this source

Android blocks installs from outside the Play Store by default. To unlock it for this one download:

  • Open your phone's Settings
  • Search for "Install unknown apps" or go to Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps
  • Find your browser (e.g. Chrome) in the list and toggle it on
💡 You can turn this off again after installing Frictionizer — it only needs to be on for the install itself.
3

Open the downloaded file

Go to your Downloads folder (or pull down the notification shade — the download should appear there) and tap frictionizer.apk. Tap Install when prompted.

4

Enable the Accessibility Service

Open Frictionizer. You'll see a banner saying the service isn't running yet. Tap Enable in Accessibility Settings, find Frictionizer in the list, and turn it on.

🔒 This is the permission that lets Frictionizer detect when you open an app. It's the same kind of permission used by password managers and screen readers. No data leaves your device — see below.
5

Choose your apps and activities

Back in Frictionizer, tap Select apps to choose which apps trigger the prompt. Common time-wasters — Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, TikTok and others — are pre-listed if you have them installed. You can also edit the five activity labels to match how you actually spend your time.

Permissions & privacy

What it asks for, and why.

Frictionizer asks for two permissions. That's it.

♿

Accessibility Service

Detects when you open a monitored app, so the prompt can appear. The service only reads the app name — it cannot see what's on screen or read what you type.

📋

Query Installed Apps

Lists the apps on your phone so you can choose which ones to monitor. The list never leaves your device.

🔒 No internet access. No account required. No analytics. Everything — your app list, your usage stats, your choices — stays on your phone.
